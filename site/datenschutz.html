3. Speicherung im Browser

Die Seite merkt sich einige Einstellungen im lokalen Speicher deines Browsers (localStorage):

  • ro-lang – die gewählte Sprache der Startseite,
  • vitepress-theme-appearance – helles oder dunkles Design (Startseite und Dokumentation),
  • vitepress:local-search-detailed-list – ob die Suche der Dokumentation Details anzeigt.

Die Suche der Dokumentation läuft vollständig in deinem Browser; Suchbegriffe werden weder gespeichert noch übertragen. Alle Werte bleiben auf deinem Gerät und werden nicht übertragen – weder an mich noch an GitHub.

Die Speicherung ist unbedingt erforderlich, um die von dir ausdrücklich gewählte Einstellung bereitzustellen (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG). Du kannst sie jederzeit über die Einstellungen deines Browsers löschen.

Privacy policy

In short: this website sets no cookies, uses no tracking and loads nothing from third-party servers. Personal data is only processed by GitHub for hosting.

This policy covers the website m4rcelnoel.github.io/ring-off, including the documentation at /ring-off/docs/. The Ring Off software itself runs on your own server – I receive no data from it at all.

Marcel Gruber (private open-source project)

2. Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you open the site, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular:

  • IP address
  • date and time of access

  • browser type and version, operating system (user agent)
  • previously visited page (referrer), if your browser sends it

Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is to provide the website securely, reliably and free of charge.

More information: GitHub's privacy statement and data collection on GitHub Pages.

3. Storage in your browser

The site remembers a few settings in your browser's local storage (localStorage):

  • ro-lang – the language you chose on the home page,
  • vitepress-theme-appearance – light or dark theme (home page and documentation),
  • vitepress:local-search-detailed-list – whether the documentation search shows details.

The documentation search runs entirely in your browser; search terms are neither stored nor transmitted. All values stay on your device and are never transmitted – neither to me nor to GitHub.

This storage is strictly necessary to provide the setting you explicitly chose (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

4. No cookies, no tracking, no external content

This website uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking tools and no advertising. Fonts, scripts and images are loaded only from this website itself, never from third parties.

5. Links to other websites

The site contains links, for example to GitHub. You only leave this website when you click a link; the target site's own privacy policy then applies.

6. Contact by email

If you email me, I process your address and message only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The message is deleted once the matter is settled, unless legal retention duties apply.

7. Your rights

Within the limits of the law you have the right to

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17) and restriction of processing (Art. 18),
  • data portability (Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21),
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77).

To make a request, simply email ring-off@buddster.de. Please note that I do not store any access logs myself; data processed by GitHub for hosting is held by GitHub.
